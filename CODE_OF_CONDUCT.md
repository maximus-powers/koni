# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community free from harassment for everyone.

We pledge to act in ways that support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes:

- showing empathy and kindness;
- respecting different opinions and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing private information without permission;
- other behavior that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders will clarify and enforce these standards. They can remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate these standards.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in community spaces. It also applies when a person represents the
community in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through a
[private GitHub report](https://github.com/maximus-powers/koni/security/advisories/new).

Community leaders will protect the privacy and security of each reporter.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders will use these responses:

1. Correction. A private warning explains the behavior and the required correction.
2. Warning. Continued behavior has stated consequences and can restrict contact.
3. Temporary ban. A serious violation can result in a temporary communication ban.
4. Permanent ban. Repeated or severe violations can result in a permanent ban.
